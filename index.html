Live product

Open Boat: shared seats on offshore charters

Anglers book one seat on a shared trip instead of a whole boat. The promise: a trip confirms only when enough seats fill, with a full refund if it doesn't.

I built the whole stack: the site and custom domain, angler and captain forms, an Airtable base with four linked tables, and the Make scenarios that connect them.

What I learned: after outreach to more than 30 captains, the pattern was clear. Established captains rarely have empty seats. Newer, smaller captains do. That changed who the product is for before any money went into ads.

How I work

Three rules I hold myself to on every build.

Every task becomes a system

If I do something twice, I ask what reusable piece it should become. You end up with a setup that keeps working, not a favor that needs repeating.

Proof before "done"

A step isn't finished until I can show it: a test submission, the row that appeared, the email that arrived. You get the evidence with the handoff.

Less for you to touch

I measure success by how little you have to do afterward. Fewer check-ins, fewer manual copies, fewer dropped leads.
